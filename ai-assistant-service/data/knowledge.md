# Game Item Pricing & Catalog Knowledge Base

## Pricing principles
Game item prices are driven by rarity, wear/float, market supply, and recent
sale velocity. A fair asking price is typically within 5-10% of the median of the
last 20 comparable sales. Items that have been listed for a long time should be
priced toward the lower end of that band.

## CS2 knife skins
Knife skins are premium items. Factory New and StatTrak variants command a
significant premium (often 30-60% over Field-Tested). For a mid-tier knife, a
reasonable range is 300-800 depending on pattern and float. Prices above 1000
usually require rare patterns (e.g. low-float fades or blue gems).

## CS2 rifle skins
Rifle skins such as AK-47 and M4A4 are the most liquid category. Popular skins
like Redline or Asiimov trade quickly; price them near the market median for a
fast sale. Field-Tested is the most common wear and the most liquid.

## Account and top-up items
Accounts are higher-risk and should be escrow-protected. Price is driven by
rank, inventory value, and included rare items. Top-up/point-card items have
thin margins and should be priced close to face value.

## Selling tips
To sell quickly: price at or slightly below the median, use accurate wear/float
in the SKU spec, and keep the listing on-shelf. To maximize price: wait for a
supply dip and highlight rare attributes in the item description.
